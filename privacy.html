Privacy Policy

Stream Snacks · Effective November 20, 2026

Whose data

Stream Snacks is used only by its owner, with the owner's own accounts. It does not collect data from viewers or from other TikTok or YouTube users.

What it receives from TikTok

When the owner signs in with TikTok, the tool receives that account's basic profile information (its ID, display name and profile picture) and an access token. It uses them only to upload Stream Snacks clips to that same account as drafts, for the owner to review and post in the TikTok app.

How it is stored

The access token is kept only on the owner's own computer, in a private file, and is never sold, shared or sent anywhere except TikTok. No analytics or advertising trackers are used.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time in the TikTok app, under Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions. Deleting the token file on the computer also removes it.

Changes

This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version.
