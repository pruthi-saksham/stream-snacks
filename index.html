STREAM SNACKS

The best live stream moments, bite-sized.

Stream Snacks posts short, edited highlight clips from Grand Theft Auto VI live streams on YouTube Shorts and TikTok: the crashes, the chaos and the streamers' best reactions, each one cut, captioned and credited to the streamer it came from.

The clips are made and published by the Stream Snacks owner with a small tool that runs on the owner's own computer. It uploads finished clips only to the Stream Snacks accounts.
